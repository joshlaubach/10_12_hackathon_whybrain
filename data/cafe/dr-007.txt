Title: Switch to compostable cups
Author: Sam Okafor
Status: Withdrawn
Created: 2024-08-26
Discussions-To: #cafe-decisions (team chat)

Context
=======
Customers had asked about the plastic-lined takeaway cups, and the team wanted a greener option. We found a compostable cup that looked like it would suit our drinks.

Decision
========
Move all takeaway drinks to compostable cups as soon as the first order arrives. This proposal was withdrawn before it took effect and the café kept its existing cups.

Why
===
The supplier could not deliver the cups in the quantity or on the schedule we needed. Without a reliable supply we would have run out mid-week. We chose to withdraw rather than switch back and forth. The idea can come back if a supplier is found who can deliver reliably.
